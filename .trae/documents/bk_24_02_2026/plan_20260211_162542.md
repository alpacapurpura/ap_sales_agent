### `BrandStudioLayout` Refactoring
- Add `selectedItem` state to track which specific item (Avatar, Team Member, Authority Item) is being edited.
- Update `handleEdit` to accept an optional `item` payload.
- Pass `selectedItem` to `EditSheetManager`.
