# Hybrid Brand Preview Strategy

I will implement the **Hybrid Approach** as suggested ("Header/Footer branded, Body system-clean") to resolve the visual inconsistency and ensure professional readability.

## 1. Refactor `ThemeInjector` (Selective Injection)
Modify `src/features/brand/components/visuals/theme-injector.tsx` to be "safer":
- **Inject:** Fonts, `--primary`, `--primary-foreground`, `--ring`.
- **Remove:** Overrides for `--background`, `--card`, `--foreground`, `--muted`.
- **Effect:** The app's body will remain clean (System Dark/Light), but all interactive elements (buttons, links, rings) and typography will match the client's brand.

## 2. Refactor `BrandBookPreview` (Container Cleanup)
Update `src/features/brand/components/brand-book-preview.tsx`:
- Remove any inline `style={{ backgroundColor... }}` from the main wrapper.
- Ensure it relies on the standard `bg-background` class.

## 3. Polish Sub-Sections (Accent Integration)
Update `VisualIdentitySection`, `BrandTrustSection`, `BrandVoiceSection`, `TeamGridSection`:
- Ensure headings use `font-heading` (already done mostly).
- Ensure section icons/titles use `text-primary` to tie them visually to the brand without changing the card background.
- Remove any forced `bg-card` overrides, letting them be natural system cards.

This strategy ensures the "Hero" and "Footer" pop with the client's full identity (Chameleon), while the complex data sections remain legible and structured using the application's native design system, accented by the client's primary color.
