## 1. PR summary

**Hotfix service-story** (not traditional PR). Story `app-shell-sidebar-copilot-decoupling` (outcome: `growth-copilot-layout-unification`, story 1/3).

**Problem (live repro evidence 2026-05-07):** Content area starvation + mutex absence + offset SSoT drift + mobile gaps.
- **Main content (flex-1 min-w-0) shrinks to 52px worst-case** (768px viewport × expanded sidebar × copilot open) — catastrophic.
- **AppSidebar.isCollapsed and CopilotStore.isOpen independent** — no mutex policy. At <1280px, both-open causes content death.
- **useCopilotOffset hook returns 380/60/0; CopilotSidebar grid renders 460/680/60** — 80-220px drift. All dialogs mis-centered.
- **Mobile <768:** AppSidebar lacks left drawer (only 40px topbar hamburger sin aria-label), no FAB to reopen copilot.

**NOT a z-index overlap bug** (live repro confirmed overlap=0px across all measured cells).
