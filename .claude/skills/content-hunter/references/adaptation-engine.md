# Adaptation Engine

Reference material for Phase 4 (Adapt). Every reference passes through these filters before becoming a brief.

---

## 1. Six Rules of "Steal Like an Artist" (Applied to Content)

Based on Austin Kleon's principles, adapted for digital content creation.

| # | Rule | Application | Red Flag |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Steal structure, not content** | Copy the hook type, arc shape, pacing, visual rhythm. Replace every topic-specific element. | If someone from the original niche would recognize it, you copied too much. |
| 2 | **Steal from niches your audience doesn't follow** | A fitness coach's audience doesn't follow SaaS marketing. Steal SaaS content structures for fitness. | Stealing from your own niche = your audience has already seen it. |
| 3 | **Combine 2+ references** | Merge the hook from Reference A with the arc from Reference B and the CTA style from Reference C. | Single-source adaptation is thin. Minimum 2 inputs per output. |
| 4 | **Inject your brand narrative** | Every adapted piece must connect to at least one StoryBrand element (hero problem, guide authority, transformation). | Content that doesn't sound like the brand = wasted reach. |
| 5 | **If a competitor already did it — SKIP** | Search competitor feeds for the same structure. If found in last 90 days, abandon or apply Rule 3 heavily. | "Inspired by" within same niche = audience sees both and judges. |
| 6 | **Transform through your lens** | The final piece should feel like it could ONLY come from this brand. Voice, examples, metaphors — all brand-native. | If you swap the logo and it could be anyone's content, the adaptation failed. |

## 2. SCAMPER for Content

Apply 2-3 operations per reference. Each operation transforms the original into something new.

| Operation | What It Does | Content Example |
|---|---|---|
| **S**ubstitute | Replace the topic/niche while keeping the structure | A viral cooking "5 mistakes" reel → "5 mistakes new coaches make pricing their first offer" |
| **C**ombine | Merge two content structures into one | Tutorial cascade hook + transformation arc = "I tried [method] for 30 days — here's the step-by-step and result" |
| **A**dapt | Adjust for a different culture, language, or platform | A US LinkedIn carousel → an IG Reel for LatAm audience (visual storytelling > text-heavy slides) |
| **M**odify | Change format, length, or emphasis | A 3-minute YouTube video → a 30-second Reel focusing only on the climax moment |
| **P**ut to other use | Shift the funnel stage (awareness ↔ consideration ↔ conversion) | An awareness myth-busting post → a conversion post that busts the "I can't afford it" objection |
| **E**liminate | Remove elements to simplify for production constraints | A multi-camera interview setup → a single selfie-cam talking head with text overlays (Phone Camera Rule) |
| **R**everse | Flip the perspective or expected outcome | "How I gained 10K followers" → "How I LOST 5K followers and why it was the best thing for my business" |

**SCAMPER workflow:**
1. Pick the reference
2. Choose 2-3 operations (minimum)
3. Apply sequentially — each operation builds on the previous
4. Validate result against the 6 Steal Rules
5. Check Phone Camera Rule

## 3. Validation Checklist

Every adapted idea must pass ALL five checks before becoming a brief.

| # | Check | Question | If NO |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Phone Camera Rule** | Can the creator produce this with smartphone + Canva + natural light + 30 minutes? | Simplify production. Apply SCAMPER: Eliminate. |
| 2 | **Voice Consistency** | Does this sound like the brand's voice_tone? Would the creator actually say these words? | Rewrite copy through brand voice filter. |
| 3 | **Real Pain/Desire** | Does this address a documented pain_point or desire from Brand/Offer data? | Connect to specific marketing_pain_point or marketing_desire. |
| 4 | **CTA Alignment** | Does the CTA match the funnel stage? (Awareness = follow/save, Consideration = comment/DM, Conversion = link/buy) | Adjust CTA to match content objective. |
| 5 | **Competitor Clear** | Has NO direct competitor posted this exact structure in the last 90 days? | Apply Rule 5 (SKIP) or Rule 3 (Combine heavily). |
