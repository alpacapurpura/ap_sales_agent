## Interaction States

### Loading State
- 4 Skeleton elements: `h-6 w-48`, `h-32 w-full`, `h-6 w-48`, `h-32 w-full`
- Matches SalesDetail and OpportunityDetail loading pattern

### Error State
- Single muted paragraph in `p-4`
- Copy defined in Copywriting Contract below

### Empty State (No Customers Post-Sale)
- Centered layout with `py-12 px-4 text-center`
- Heading + body + action link (matches SalesDetail empty state pattern)

### Health Bar Interaction
- Static proportional bar (no hover interaction in v1)
- Segments are CSS width percentages of container
- Minimum segment width: 4px (so a 1% segment is still visible)

### Tooltip Hints (Expansion KPIs)
- Triggered on hover/focus of Info icon next to KPI label
- Uses shadcn Tooltip component (Radix)
- 200ms open delay, 12px body text, max-width 240px
- Content: plain-Spanish explanation of the metric

### Bottleneck Banners
- Adoption: Yellow severity when overall health % < 70%
- Adoption inline: Badge on individual offer cards with health < 60%
- Expansion: Red severity when churn rate > 5%
- Context-aware tips rotate based on data pattern (see Copywriting Contract)

## Bottleneck Threshold Calibration

Based on industry research for micro-businesses and solopreneurs:

| Metric | Threshold | Severity | Rationale |
|--------|-----------|----------|-----------|
| Adoption health % | < 70% | warning (yellow) | 43% of SMB customer losses occur in first 90 days; 70% keeps a buffer above the danger zone |
| Adoption per-offer health | < 60% | inline badge | Tighter threshold for individual offers — signals product-specific issue |
| Expansion churn rate | > 5% monthly | critical (red) | SMB SaaS average is 3-5% monthly; above 5% is critical for micro-businesses with small customer bases |
| Expansion churn rate | > 3% monthly | warning (yellow) | Early warning before hitting critical threshold |

Sources: [Vitally B2B SaaS Churn Benchmarks](https://www.vitally.io/post/saas-churn-benchmarks), [Recurly Churn Rate Benchmarks](https://recurly.com/research/churn-rate-benchmarks/), [Focus Digital Retention Rates 2026](https://focus-digital.co/average-customer-retention-rate-by-industry/)

## Time-to-Value (TTV) Specification

**Calculation:** Days from `first_conversion_at` to first `journey_event` after purchase date.

**Display:**
- Adoption header secondary KPI: average TTV across all customers in period
- Per-offer card: average TTV for that offer's customers
- Format: "{N} dias" (integer, rounded)
- If no post-purchase events exist for a customer, that customer is excluded from TTV average (not counted as infinite)

**Benchmarks for context-aware tips:**
- TTV < 3 days: Excellent (no tip needed)
- TTV 3-7 days: Normal (no tip needed)
- TTV > 7 days: Slow activation — triggers onboarding-related bottleneck tip

Rationale: For micro-business infoproducts (courses, coaching, memberships), customers should engage within the first week. Beyond 7 days without engagement, the product risks feeling abandoned.

## Adoption MiniFunnel Decision

**Decision: Include MiniFunnel for Adoption.**

Rationale: The MiniFunnel component is already built and the data maps naturally: Ventas (N) -> Activos (M) = X% activacion. This gives the business owner immediate context on what percentage of buyers actually engage with their product, which is the core question of the Adoption stage. The component adds minimal complexity (reuse, no modification) and high informational value.
